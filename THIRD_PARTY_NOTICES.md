# Third-party components

- dlmserve: https://github.com/iOptimizeThings/dlmserve, MIT. Used as a pinned
  external dependency; its license and copyright remain in its source tree.
  ShapeShift's scheduler is newly implemented; upstream model loading and
  denoising are not represented as original ShapeShift code.
- dInfer and Fast-dLLM: referenced by historical scripts, not bundled. Consult
  the exact upstream version and notices before installing or redistributing.
- `paper/llncs.cls` and `paper/splncs04.bst`: Springer LNCS publication templates supplied with the camera-ready source.
  Preserve their embedded notices; the project MIT license does not replace them.
- Model checkpoints and datasets are not bundled. Their own terms apply.
- Manuscript content and archived experimental summaries are research artifacts;
  no third-party publication rights are granted by the code license.

- Archived AAAI `.sty`/`.bst` files retain their original embedded notices;
  preserving these templates does not relicense them under the project MIT license.
